Associate Professor & Doctoral Supervisor, College of Atmospheric Sciences, Lanzhou University.

**Research Interests:** Semi-arid climate change, Tibetan Plateau water cycle, extreme temperature events

Yongli He received his B.S. (2007–2011) from the College of Atmospheric Sciences, Lanzhou University, followed by a combined M.S./Ph.D. program (2011–2017) at the same institution. During his doctoral studies, he spent two years (2014–2016) as a visiting scholar in the Department of Environmental Sciences at the University of Virginia, USA. After earning his Ph.D. in 2017, he joined Lanzhou University as a faculty member, serving as a Lecturer (2017.7–2021.12) and then as an Associate Professor (2022–present).

His research focuses on mid-to-high latitude climate dynamics, with notable contributions to understanding mechanisms of enhanced semi-arid warming and the Tibetan Plateau water cycle. In recent years, he has investigated the response of the water cycle to warming, and the influence of moist physical processes on atmospheric blocking and extreme temperature events. He has published 71 papers (66 SCI papers, 25 as first/corresponding author), and has led one NSFC Young Scientist Project and one General Project. He has received the Second-class Prize for Natural Science from the Ministry of Education (ranked 4th). He serves as Deputy Secretary-General of the Youth Editorial Board of *Plateau Meteorology*, Youth Editor of *The Innovation Geoscience*, Guest Editor of *Remote Sensing*, and Associate Editor of *Discover Atmosphere*.
